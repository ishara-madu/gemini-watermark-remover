Visible Logo vs SynthID Digital Watermark: What's the difference?

Understanding Google's two-layer AI protection mechanism

1. Visible Star Logo

The semi-transparent white 4-point logo rendered in the corner of images and Veo videos. This is what our tool removes mathematically with zero quality loss.

2. SynthID (Imperceptible Metadata)

An invisible cryptographic watermark developed by Google DeepMind embedded directly into image pixel noise and audio tracks for AI detection. It does not affect visible image aesthetics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free, client-side Gemini AI watermark removal tool.

01 How does Gemini Watermark Remover work?
Google Gemini embeds a transparent logo mask into generated images and Veo videos using alpha blending. Instead of guessing pixels like AI object removers, our tool applies the exact reverse mathematical equation Original = (Watermarked - Logo * Alpha) / (1 - Alpha) to unblend the watermark pixel-by-pixel, restoring 100% of your original pixel clarity.
02 Can I turn off watermarks directly in Google Gemini settings?
Yes! On Gemini web (gemini.google.com), you can go to Settings > Media watermark and turn visible watermarks Off. However, this only prevents watermarks on future image creations and does not remove them from already saved files or Veo videos, which is where our tool comes in.
03 Does this tool support Google Gemini Imagen 3 and Veo 3 videos?
Yes! It fully supports both Google Imagen 3 images (PNG, JPG, WebP) and Gemini Veo 3 AI videos (MP4, WebM, MOV) with full audio preservation and GPU-accelerated WebCodecs.
04 What is the difference between visual watermarks and SynthID?
Visual watermarks are the transparent 4-point sparkle logos seen in the corner of images and videos. SynthID is an imperceptible digital cryptographic watermark embedded directly into pixel noise by Google DeepMind for AI detection that does not affect visual appearance.
05 Is this tool completely free to use?
Yes, 100% free! There are no hidden subscription fees, no user registration required, no file count limits, and no secondary watermarks added to your exported files.
06 Are my images and videos kept private and secure?
Absolutely. All image and video processing is executed 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebCodecs APIs. Your files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external server.
07 Why is mathematical removal better than AI inpainting?
AI inpainting tools guess what lies beneath the watermark by blurring or smudging pixels, causing loss of detail. Mathematical alpha unblending subtracts the exact transparency values of the watermark, revealing the actual sharp original pixels underneath.
08 What image and video file formats are supported?
We support all major formats: Images (PNG, JPG, WebP) and Videos (MP4, WebM, MOV) with instant download options.
09 What if the watermark box is slightly misaligned?
You can use the built-in Live Tuner controls (Strength/Gain, Size Scale, Position X, and Position Y sliders). The dual zoomed preview windows allow you to visually align the box over the watermark with pixel perfection.
10 Can I use this tool on mobile phones and tablets?
Yes! The website is fully mobile-responsive and works seamlessly on smartphones and tablets across iOS (Safari) and Android (Chrome/Edge/Firefox).
